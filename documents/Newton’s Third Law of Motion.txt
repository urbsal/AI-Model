Newton’s Third Law of Motion

Newton’s Third Law states:

“For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.”

This means that when one object exerts a force on another object, the second object exerts an equal force in the opposite direction on the first object.

Example: When you push against a wall, you apply a force to the wall. The wall pushes back on you with an equal force in the opposite direction.

Another example is walking. Your foot pushes the ground backward, and the ground pushes your foot forward, allowing you to move forward.
